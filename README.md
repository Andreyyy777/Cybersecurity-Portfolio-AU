# Cybersecurity Portfolio (SOC / Threat Intelligence Pathway)

## Overview

This repository contains structured analytical simulations and conceptual models demonstrating investigative thinking applied to cybersecurity.

The focus is on:

- MITRE ATT&CK-based attack chain reasoning  
- Context-aware risk evaluation  
- Incident escalation logic  
- Behavioral fingerprint comparison  
- Campaign correlation methodology  

All case studies represent independent analytical simulations designed to demonstrate investigation structure, reasoning, and documentation discipline.

---

## Core Areas Covered

### 1. Attack Chain Analysis
Structured breakdown of multi-stage intrusion scenarios including:
- Initial Access
- Execution
- Persistence
- Credential Access
- Lateral Movement
- Collection & Exfiltration

Focus: correlation vs isolated indicators.

---

### 2. Context-Aware Risk Modeling
Conceptual detection framework combining:
- Indicator severity
- Behavioral chain continuity
- Asset criticality
- Spread potential
- Baseline deviation

Emphasis on human-in-the-loop escalation validation.

---

### 3. Campaign Correlation Thinking
Methodology for distinguishing:
- Infrastructure reuse
- Behavioral similarity
- Operational tempo correlation

Demonstrates transition from SOC-level investigation to threat intelligence reasoning.

---
